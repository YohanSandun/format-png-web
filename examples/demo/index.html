format-png

A PNG decoder and encoder in WebAssembly. Everything runs in your browser; files never leave it.

npm GitHub

Inspect every chunk

readChunks reads a PNG without decompressing its image data: the header, palette, transparency, color and text metadata parsed, and every chunk raw, including private and unknown ones.

    Decode

    readHeader reads only the signature and IHDR; decodeRgba8 decodes every color type, bit depth and interlacing to 8-bit RGBA, ready for a canvas.

    Header

    Encode

    encodeRgba8 writes any image as a PNG, then the result is decoded again to check every pixel came through. PNGs keep their metadata and chunks; JPEG, WebP and other formats are decoded by the browser. Change an option to encode again.

    Minimize

    Makes a PNG as small as possible without changing a single pixel, 16-bit included: it strips the chunks you choose, converts images with at most 256 colors to a palette, and tries several encodings in parallel to keep the smallest. Everything runs in format-png's worker pool, decoding included, so the page stays responsive even for very large images.
